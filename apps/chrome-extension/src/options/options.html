Warn about sites nobody has listed yet

The block lists are exact: a gambling site registered this morning is not on them yet. This looks at the address itself and warns when it reads like one — the page still opens, with a "continue anyway" you can click. It is a guess, so it is off unless you want it.

Warn about likely matches
Warns only. It never blocks anything on its own.
Gambling
Catches about a fifth of unlisted gambling sites, and is wrong on roughly one address in a million.
Adult
About a quarter of unlisted adult sites, and wrong somewhat more often than the gambling one.

Only these two are offered. The same approach was measured on scam, social, games and drugs and was not accurate enough to be worth the interruptions, so it is not shipped for them.

Blur photos of people

Photos are blurred where they appear, on every site. Everything runs on this device — no image is ever uploaded.

Blur videos
Videos are blurred for as long as they play — they are not checked frame by frame.

Recognising who is in a photo is imperfect. It is least reliable on children, side profiles, small thumbnails, and faces in hijab or niqab. When it is unsure it blurs, so expect some photos to be blurred that did not need to be.
